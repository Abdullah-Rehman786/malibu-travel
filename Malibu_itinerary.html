Trip strategy

One base. Mountains and ocean on the same day.

Stay in Marina del Rey for all three nights. It keeps LAX, Santa Monica, Venice and your water activities close, while Malibu and the Santa Monica Mountains remain an easy day trip west.

If snorkeling conditions are poor

Do not force open-ocean snorkeling. Switch to Point Dume beach/swimming, more time at Sycamore/Zuma, or another calm-water SUP/kayak session.

If trails close for fire weather

Santa Monica Mountains lands may close during Red Flag Warnings. Replace the hiking block with Point Dume + beaches + marina activities.

Driving rule

Do not add Hollywood, Downtown LA or another major attraction. The trip works because it stays on the Westside and along PCH. Add 15–30 minutes of traffic buffer when a reservation matters.
